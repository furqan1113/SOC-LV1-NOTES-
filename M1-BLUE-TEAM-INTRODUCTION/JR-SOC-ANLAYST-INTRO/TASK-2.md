# Task 2 – Security Operations Center (SOC)

## 1. Main Concept

A SOC is a team responsible for monitoring, detecting, investigating, and responding to cyber threats.

A SOC is a team with different roles working together to monitor, detect, investigate, and respond to security threats.

## 2. Important Things to Remember

- **SOC L1/Junior Analyst:** Monitors and performs initial investigation of alerts.
- **Senior Analyst:** Helps with unclear or complex cases and handles escalated investigations.
- **SOC Engineer:** Maintains security tools and configures/improves alerts.
- **SOC Manager:** Manages the SOC team and reports results to TOP management.
- **Incident Responder:** Handles major or serious security incidents when needed.
- SOC work requires teamwork, communication, and escalation.

## 3. What I Should Understand / Be Able to Do After This Task

Understand who does what in a SOC, know when a case may need to be escalated, and understand how a SOC L1 works with other security roles during normal alerts and serious incidents.
