# SOC Quick Reference — Q&A

- **Where does SOC fit?** → Blue Team / defensive security.
- **What does L1 do?** → Monitor, triage, initially investigate, and escalate complex cases.
- **What happens to more complex cases?** → L2 investigates them.
- **What about major incidents?** → CIRT/CSIRT may become involved.
- **What does a SOC engineer do?** → Maintains/configures security tools and improves detection.
- **Where can an L1 career go?** → L2 or specialized defensive/security roles.
- **Internal SOC vs MSSP?** → One organization's environment vs security services for multiple clients, generally with different pace and exposure.
